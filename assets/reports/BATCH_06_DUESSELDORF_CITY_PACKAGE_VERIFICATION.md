# Batch 06 — Düsseldorf premium city package verification

## Result

The sixth premium city package is approved and runtime-integrated. It contains
eight coordinated, text-free assets:

- 21:9 day and night heroes,
- 16:9 day and night desktop heroes,
- separately composed and revised 9:16 day and night mobile heroes,
- one separately composed and revised 1:1 city card,
- one deterministic transparent skyline silhouette.

The package evokes a design-conscious services, fashion, trade, and media
metropolis through a broad fictional river, original low-profile bridge,
landscaped public promenade, small original canal basin, refined pale-stone
district, and restrained glass-and-metal offices. It does not reproduce the
Rheinturm, MedienHafen or Gehry buildings, Königsallee, Kö-Bogen, a known
Düsseldorf bridge, fashion or media brand, trade-fair structure, transport
plan, authority, or photograph.

## Review corrections

### Mobile day hero

The first portrait recomposition was held back before ingestion because an
isolated narrow pointed silhouette on the distant skyline could read as a real
church, cathedral, civic tower, or antenna landmark.

The accepted revision:

- replaces the isolated point with modest neutral flat-roofed development,
- retains the calm upper 32% status/title zone,
- preserves the central canal-basin and promenade decision area,
- retains the original low-profile bridge,
- keeps the lower 18% as calm dark open water for one primary control.

### Square card

The first square recomposition was held back because a single distant needle or
spire could create a religious, civic, or recognizable antenna association.

The accepted revision replaces it with neutral original stone-and-glass urban
blocks. The complete lower 24% remains uninterrupted calm dark river water for
external localized labels, badges, and one action.

Only the corrected mobile-day and card images were added to the production
asset store and runtime.

## Responsive composition

The desktop, mobile, and square images are genuine recompositions:

- ultra-wide: calm dark low-detail left 38% for localized interface content,
- desktop: calm dark low-detail left 32% with the riverfront, promenade, canal
  basin, and services district concentrated center-right,
- mobile: calm upper 32%, one central riverfront-services decision area, and
  quiet lower 18% for one primary action,
- square card: canal basin, pale-stone services district, promenade, and
  original bridge above a complete lower 24% calm-water label band.

The Germany page renders Köln, Hamburg, Berlin, München, Frankfurt, and
Düsseldorf from one data-driven package list. Each city exposes six responsive
AVIF sources for mobile, desktop, ultra-wide, day, and night conditions, plus
PNG fallbacks, a square card, and a transparent silhouette.
